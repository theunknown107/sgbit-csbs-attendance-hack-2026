## 🌟 The Experience

1. **The Bait (Scan for Free Attendance):**  
   Posters placed across campus say *"SCAN FOR Free Attendance - Scan to check in"* with a high-contrast QR code.
2. **The Portal (Realistic VTU Check-In):**  
   When scanned, students open a hyper-clean, authentic-looking institutional attendance verification portal asking for their **USN**, **Full Name**, and **Division**.
3. **The Suspense:**  
   When hitting *"Claim Free Attendance"*, the portal runs a sleek simulated biometric check querying the VTU student database.
4. **The Drop (Prank Reveal & Celebration):**  
   BAM! Confetti explosion, celebratory sound effects, and the hilarious reveal:  
   > *"WAIT... DID YOU ACTUALLY THINK ATTENDANCE WAS FREE?! 😂💀 There's no free attendance... BUT YOU JUST UNLOCKED VIP ACCESS TO AAGAMAN 2K26!"*
5. **The VIP Ticket & Event Showcase:**  
   - Interactive 3D tilt showcase of the official **Aagaman 2K26** poster.
   - Dynamically generated personalized **VIP Fresher's Admit Pass** featuring the student's actual Name, USN, Division, unique serial code, and barcode.
   - Instant 1-click **Download VIP Pass** (exports high-res 1200x630 PNG).
   - Real-time countdown timer to **30th November 2026**.
   - 1-click WhatsApp and clipboard viral prank sharing buttons.
